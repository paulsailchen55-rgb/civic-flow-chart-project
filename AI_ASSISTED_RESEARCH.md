# AI-Assisted Research

This project uses AI as a research, drafting, modeling, and testing tool. Because the project can also be used to study AI-mediated systems, AI interactions that materially change the model may be recorded as anonymized experience records.

## Current case study

- [AI-Assisted Civic Flow Experience — Anonymized Case Study](docs/AI_ASSISTED_CIVIC_FLOW_EXPERIENCE.md)

## Recording rule

AI-assisted experience records should distinguish:

**HUMAN OBSERVATION → AI-ASSISTED INTERPRETATION → REAL-WORLD ACTION → OBSERVED RESPONSE → MODEL REVISION**

The AI output is not automatically evidence of an external fact. External claims should be verified against appropriate sources.

## Privacy rule

Public records should omit unnecessary personal identifiers, private contact information, account identifiers, case numbers, and details that could unfairly expose an individual. Anonymization is not assumed to guarantee privacy; re-identification risk should be considered before publication.

## Design rule

The Civic Flow Chart should never require AI merely to understand or exercise a public process. AI may assist the map; it does not become the authority represented by the map.
