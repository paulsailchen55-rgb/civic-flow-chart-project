CIVIC RESEARCH SANDBOX

Community Questions & Source Audit

Research flow: Question → Preliminary answer → Source → Check the source → Who can answer/verify? → Who makes the decision? → What remains unknown

What this is

This is an open working template for turning community questions into research that other people can check.

A concern or reported statement is not automatically a verified fact. A preliminary answer is not automatically verified. A source being listed is not the same as the source having been checked.

This template is designed to work without requiring a person's name, organization, or private conversation to appear in the public research record.

Document information

How to use this sandbox

1. Ask a question. Start with what needs to be understood.

2. Record a preliminary answer. Make the current understanding visible, but label it preliminary.

3. Find the source. Identify the document, record, rule, agency, or other source that should establish the answer.

4. Check the source. Open it and ask whether it actually supports the statement.

5. Identify authority. Separate the person or agency that can answer or verify from the person or body that actually makes the decision.

6. Record uncertainty. UNKNOWN, NOT YET MAPPED, PARTLY VERIFIED, and DISPUTED are legitimate results.

7. Update. Correct the record when better information becomes available.

Overall research audit

What do we think? What did somebody tell us? What does the source actually say? What can we verify? What remains unknown?

An “unverified” or “unknown until later” result is legitimate. The purpose is to make the path from question to answer—and from error to correction—visible and easy for someone else to follow.

Anonymous participation

A question can be entered without identifying the person who raised it. If a source is relevant, record the source itself rather than unnecessary personal information. Private conversations should not be reproduced publicly without permission.

Open working template. Not an official government document. Verify current information against the authoritative source before relying on it. The template itself is open to correction and improvement.
